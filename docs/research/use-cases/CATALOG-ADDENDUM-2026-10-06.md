## Priority from the 2026-10-06 batch
1. **UC-169** — strongest real-network experiment because it turns student mobility into a decentralized routing signal while explicitly limiting tracking.
2. **UC-168** — strongest reliability case because forwarding and responsibility are separated and can be tested with real store-and-forward failures.
3. **UC-171** — strongest new Messina field scenario because it couples coastal sensing, delayed evidence and physical/data-mule transport.

UC-170 is the strongest security-migration case. UC-172 is a cross-cutting data-management primitive for long-running offline applications.

## Physical-validation reminder
No entry changes the frozen LoRa PHY. Range, PDR, RSSI/SNR interpretation, throughput, contact duration, energy, application reliability and calibration quality remain real-hardware measurement questions.
