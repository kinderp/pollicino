## Top three
1. **UC-196** — independently observe the same exact radio test transmissions at two fixed, authorized receivers. Software: correlated-loss and missing-log simulator. Hardware: 1 TX, 2 RX, 4–6 student relays, verified sender denominator. Key result to measure: receiver-specific loss and union gain, not a coverage estimate.
2. **UC-198** — show that a cryptographically valid but stale intent is not executed. Software: bounded state machine with partition/replay/clock skew. Hardware: 4–6 boards with a low-voltage LED/servo and independent manual disconnect. Measure executed effects vs rejected old commands.
3. **UC-197** — quality-controlled hill microclimate observations with delayed review. Software: missing/stale/biased synthetic sensor series. Hardware: supervised tabletop sensors, then only permissioned fixed stations; no real wildfire alert functionality.

UC-199 (intermediate AI features and privacy risk) and UC-200 (tabletop emergency exercise reconstruction) remain software-first.

## Safety and audit gates
Catalog UC-001–UC-200 is contiguous. Every new UC documents actors, transport mapping, simulation plan, physical measurements, privacy/safety and nonduplication. New/edited paths are documentation only under this directory. Student routes, homes and personal data remain excluded; field radio power/airtime compliance must be checked for the specific sub-band.
