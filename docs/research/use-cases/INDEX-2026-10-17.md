## Priorities
1. UC-217: an offline stop for voluntary network experiments.
2. UC-216: reproducible synthetic data for classroom research without releasing raw encounter traces.
3. UC-218: measured sustained edge AI reliability before deployment.

The remaining two are useful but secondary research demonstrations. Start with supervised lab stations representing Messina, Villafranca, Rometta and Venetico. All tests should use artificial participants and data first. Physical results such as reception, throughput, contact duration and energy require actual instruments and measurements. No radio PHY/MAC changes, emergency certifications or completed experiments are claimed.

References: RFC 9171 (https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9171) and ggwave (https://github.com/ggerganov/ggwave).
