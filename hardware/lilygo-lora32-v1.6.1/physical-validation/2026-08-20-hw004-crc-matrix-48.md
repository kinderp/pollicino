# HW-004 physical CRC/reliability matrix — 2026-08-20

## Scope

Same-bench exploratory reliability matrix using the already validated HW-003 event-driven firmware. No firmware, H2 wire format, or PHY change was made for HW-004.

- Port A: COM3
- Port B: COM4
- PHY: 868.100 MHz, BW 125 kHz, SF7, CR 4/5, 10 dBm
- Frame sizes: 16, 32, 42, 60, 120, 240 bytes
- Four counterbalanced rounds
- 48 attempts total; four attempts per direction × size cell
- Experimental pacing input: 1% TX occupancy cap
- Planned per-node TX airtime: 7.24 s
- Measured wall time: 688.625 s

All 48 attempts succeeded and all 48 responder timing traces matched.

The responder task wait and wake counters advanced by exactly one for all 48 attempts. There were zero spurious-wake deltas and zero mutex-timeout deltas. IRQ-to-handler timing remained 16..23 us overall (mean 16.3125 us, p95 17 us).

## Interpretation

This matrix supplies no evidence for a persistent COM4 -> COM3 CRC defect and no evidence for an obvious frame-size-associated failure pattern under the present same-bench conditions. The immediately preceding same-direction 42-byte repeat was also 20/20, so the two CRC observations from the earlier mirror run have not reproduced in the subsequent controlled work.

The correct conclusion is **intermittent/unresolved CRC observations**, not "CRC fixed" and not "zero packet-loss probability". With zero failures in HW-004, the matrix cannot estimate a direction or size effect; it only weakens hypotheses that should have produced repeatable failures in these cells.

The scheduling question remains separate and closed at the present bench scope: event-driven wake/handler behavior stayed stable for both physical responder boards and across the full 16..240 byte frame-size range.

## Secondary timing observation

The cross-node RTT residual changes systematically with frame size in this run (roughly -254 us at 16 B, ~3 us at 32 B, 16 us at 42 B, 150 us at 60 B, 519 us at 120 B, and 985 us at 240 B). This is retained as a diagnostic observation only. The residual combines independently clocked local intervals and uninstrumented radio/driver boundaries and must not be interpreted as propagation delay. A dedicated timing-boundary experiment would be needed before assigning that size trend to any physical cause.

## Boundaries

- Success fractions here are descriptive bench counts, not deployment packet-loss probabilities.
- No claim is made about range, NLOS, interference tolerance, or field reliability.
- RSSI/SNR are available only for successfully decoded frames in the current firmware.
- Scheduler counters are not electrical power/current/energy measurements.
- Cross-node RTT residual is diagnostic only and is not one-way propagation delay.
